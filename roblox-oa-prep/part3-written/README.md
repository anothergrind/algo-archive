# Part 3 — Written / Behavioural

**Time limit:** 25 minutes for all six. That is roughly **4 minutes per question**,
which is the real constraint — these are short-response boxes, not essays. Aim for
120–200 words each.

Rubrics, and a worked example of a weak answer for each, are in
[RUBRICS.md](RUBRICS.md). **Draft your answer first, then open it.**

---

## Q1 — Why Roblox?

Why do you want to work at Roblox specifically, rather than at any other technology
company of similar size?

*Your answer should be one that could not be pasted into an application for a different
company with only the name swapped.*

---

## Q2 — Situational judgement

You are three weeks into a ten-week internship project. A teammate has proposed a
design for the component you both depend on. You think it is over-engineered — it will
take an extra week to build and you do not believe the flexibility it buys will ever be
used. Your teammate has been at the company for four years. Your mentor is on holiday
until next week.

What do you do?

---

## Q3 — Situational judgement

You discover, two days before your end-of-internship presentation, that a number you
have been reporting in your weekly updates for the last month is wrong. The corrected
number makes your project look substantially less impressive, though still positive.
Nobody has noticed.

What do you do?

---

## Q4 — Trade-off

You have one week left in the internship. You can either polish the feature you have
built — fixing rough edges, adding tests, writing documentation — or start a second,
smaller feature that would visibly expand the scope of what you shipped.

How do you decide? Walk through your reasoning, not just your conclusion.

---

## Q5 — Trade-off

A system you own is slow. You can make it 10× faster for the 5% of users who hit the
worst case, or 1.2× faster for everyone. Engineering cost is roughly the same.

Which do you choose, and what would change your mind?

---

## Q6 — Tell me about a time

Tell me about a time you were wrong about something technical, and how you found out.

*Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result.*

---

## How to use this section

Answer cold and under time — the whole point of a 25-minute box is that it catches
people who have not thought about these before. When you have a draft, open
[RUBRICS.md](RUBRICS.md) and score yourself honestly against the criteria before
reading the weak-answer example, which is there to show you the specific failure mode
each question is designed to surface.
